Know before you borrow

A loan should fit your budget — not the other way around.

Before borrowing, check the total amount repayable, fees, late-payment charges, instalment dates and whether the lender is properly authorized.

FAQs

A few things worth knowing.

Can I get a loan through ApnaLoan today?

No. This is an early demonstration website. It does not issue loans, assess eligibility or forward applications to lenders.

Does completing the questions guarantee approval?

No. The questions only create an on-screen summary. Approval, rates and terms can only be determined by an authorized lender under its applicable rules.

Is my information saved?

No. Answers are held temporarily in the current page session only. They are not sent to a server or stored in a database.

How should I check a lender?

Check the relevant regulator's official website and read all loan terms and charges before sharing documents or paying any fee.
